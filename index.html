📘 Learn the rules

Bigger unit to smaller unit:
Multiply by 1,000.
2 kg = 2 × 1,000 = 2,000 g
Smaller unit to bigger unit:
Divide by 1,000.
2,000 mL = 2,000 ÷ 1,000 = 2 L

Combined measurements

3 kg 250 g into grams:
3 kg = 3,000 g
3,000 + 250 = 3,250 g
5 L 30 mL into millilitres:
5 L = 5,000 mL
5,000 + 30 = 5,030 mL

⚖️ Kilograms and grams

We use kilograms for heavier things and grams for lighter things.

1 kg = 1,000 g
3 kg 250 g = 3,250 g

💧 Litres and millilitres
