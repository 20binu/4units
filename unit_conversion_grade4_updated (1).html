We use kilograms and grams to measure how heavy something is. We use litres and millilitres to measure how much liquid a container can hold.

Two magic facts to remember:
1 kg = 1,000 g
1 L = 1,000 mL

Watch the liquid fill the bottle and the weighing scale gently move. These little animations help us remember what we measure!

📘 The big idea

Bigger unit → smaller unit: multiply by 1,000.
Example: 2 kg = 2 × 1,000 = 2,000 g
Smaller unit → bigger unit: divide by 1,000.
Example: 2,000 mL = 2,000 ÷ 1,000 = 2 L

Mixed measurements: Change each part into the smaller unit, then add.

Example: 3 kg 250 g into grams
3 kg = 3,000 g
3,000 g + 250 g = 3,250 g
Example: 5 L 30 mL into millilitres
5 L = 5,000 mL
5,000 mL + 30 mL = 5,030 mL
