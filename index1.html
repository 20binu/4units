📖 Let’s learn the easy rule

Only one important fact to remember for each pair of units.

MASS1 kg = 1,000 g1 kilogram is 1,000 grams.
CAPACITY1 L = 1,000 mL1 litre is 1,000 millilitres.

How do I change a bigger unit into a smaller unit?

🍉
2 kg × 1,000
= 2,000 g

Multiply by 1,000. Remember: kg to g and L to mL.

How do I change a smaller unit into a bigger unit?

🧴
3,000 mL ÷ 1,000
= 3 L

Divide by 1,000. Remember: g to kg and mL to L.

Let’s solve a mixed-unit example

13 kg = 3,000 g
2Keep the extra 250 g
33,000 + 250 = 3,250 g
3 kg 250 g = 3,250 g
Try this way for 5 L 30 mL: 5,000 mL + 30 mL = 5,030 mL.

L and mL

Bucket

Water bottle

Remember

1 litre (L) = 1,000 millilitres (mL)
Example: 1 L 250 mL = 1,000 mL + 250 mL = 1,250 mL.
